# Attribution

This project's curated dataset (`content/`) is built by merging several
independent, openly-licensed Swedish-language resources. Each is credited
here with its actual confirmed license — not assumed, verified against the
publisher's own pages during this project's licensing review.

## Word list, translations, examples

| Source | What we use it for | License |
|---|---|---|
| [Kelly list](https://spraakbanken.gu.se/en/resources/kelly) (Språkbanken, University of Gothenburg) | The word spine: lemma, part of speech, grammatical gender, CEFR level, frequency rank | [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) + LGPL 3.0 |
| [Folkets Lexikon](https://folkets-lexikon.csc.kth.se/folkets/) (KTH) | Translations and example sentences | [CC BY-SA 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/) |
| [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/) (via [Kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/)/[Wiktextract](https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract)) | Translations, conjugation/declension forms, IPA, example sentences | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) + GFDL (Wiktionary's own license; Kaikki.org states none of its own, but this is a mechanical extraction of Wiktionary text, which carries this license regardless of what the extraction tool's own site says) |

Citation for the Kelly list, as Språkbanken requests: Volodina, E. and
Johansson Kokkinakis, S. (2012). *Introducing the Swedish Kelly-list, a new
lexical e-resource for Swedish.* Enriched and distributed by Språkbanken
(https://doi.org/10.23695/6act-rs25).

Citation for Wiktextract, as its author requests for academic use: Ylonen, T.
(2022). *Wiktextract: Wiktionary as Machine-Readable Structured Data.*
Proceedings of LREC 2022, pp. 1317–1325.

### Sources considered and NOT used in the shipped content

- **SVALex** (Språkbanken) — licensed [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)
  (non-commercial). Used only internally during development as a secondary
  frequency cross-check; never shown on any card and deliberately excluded
  from `content/kelly_final.csv` so its non-commercial restriction never
  touches anything published.
- **SALDO/SALDOM** (Språkbanken) — [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Used only for ad hoc sense-disambiguation research while curating the
  dataset by hand; not a dependency of any committed script, so nothing from
  it is redistributed here.
